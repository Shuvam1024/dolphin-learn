## Reading

The fraction 3/4 means three equal parts out of four equal parts of one whole. The denominator names how many equal parts make the whole. The numerator counts how many of those parts you have. Equal denominators mean the parts are the same size, so you can compare or combine numerators while keeping the shared denominator. Saying the fraction in words — three fourths — is a check that the symbols still mean a part-whole relationship, not two unrelated integers sitting next to a slash.
