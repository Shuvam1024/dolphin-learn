# Dolphin — Brand shortlist (locked)

**Status:** Product name **Dolphin** is locked.  
**Date:** September 23, 2026  
**Clearance note:** Preliminary web conflict screen only — **not** legal trademark clearance. Run counsel search before public launch / domain / app-store filing.

---

## Locked name

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Product name | **Dolphin** |
| Category line | Learning platform / adaptive study home |
| Preferred tagline | *Learn anything. Fit the time you have. Prove you can do it.* |
| Visual direction | Clear Depth — deep water ink, seafoam action, foam surfaces, signal amber (see master design §9.3) |
| Do not ship as | Atlas, Coyote, Tallymark, Planhaven, Planmoor, Truepace, or other retired candidates below |

**Why Dolphin wins (product sense):** fluid movement through constrained time; depth without opacity; navigates complex waters calmly. Fits time-adaptive planning + Home as hub + honest Evidence Ledger without sounding like another indigo AI tutor or K–12 cartoon LMS. Distinct from retired Coyote (mesa/canyon) and Atlas (edtech trademark thicket).

## Conflict screen (quick web pass)

Existing uses to stay distinctive from:

| Name / product | What it is | Distancing guidance |
|---|---|---|
| **海豚AI学 / Dolphin AI Learning** (Yuanfudao / Ape Tutoring) | China K–12 (grades 1–9) AI self-study: animated lessons, Socratic Q&A, parent reports, school-synced subjects | We are **adult-first (18+)**, English-first consumer learning OS, evidence-honest, no parent dashboard / school sync positioning. Avoid lockups **“Dolphin AI Learning”**, **“海豚AI学”**, and K–12 tutoring claims. |
| **Dolphin Learning** (iF Design 2021; related animated self-study) | Animated personalized content + exercises for students | Same distancing: adult 18+, Session Studio + real-minute plans + Evidence Ledger — not animation-first school packs. |
| **SQLearn Dolphin System** | Maritime SaaS LMS / onboard training hardware (“Dolphin box”) for shipping fleets | Different category (enterprise maritime TMS). Avoid **“Dolphin System”**, fleet/vessel training messaging, and LMS-procurement tone. |
| **Dolphin Learning Solutions** (Bengaluru) | Education consulting / LMS services (NEP-aligned institutional solutions) | Different category (B2B consulting). Avoid **“Dolphin Learning Solutions”** compound; prefer **Dolphin** alone + learning tagline. |

**Other noise (lower confusion, still note):** general “dolphin” consumer brands and dead/unrelated trademarks may create SEO clutter — lean on tagline + “learning” qualifier in titles/meta.

**Recommendation:** Ship as **Dolphin** with a learning-specific tagline in product chrome and store listings. Secondary lockup: **Dolphin — Learn. Fit the time. Prove it.** Avoid Dolphin AI Learning / Dolphin System / Dolphin Learning Solutions lookalikes.

**Risk level (preliminary):** Medium in edtech globally due to established China K–12 “Dolphin” apps and maritime LMS use; mitigate with adult-first positioning, distinctive Clear Depth identity, and counsel clearance before launch.

## Naming dos / don’ts

**Do**

- Use **Dolphin** as the sole product name in docs, UI, README, tickets.
- Pair with “learning,” “study,” “Evidence Ledger,” “Session Studio,” “Home” for category clarity.
- Keep adult-first messaging; state omega vision without “all subjects fully mastered” claims.
- Cite Atlas / Coyote packages only as superseded design history.

**Don’t**

- Don’t brand as Dolphin AI Learning, Dolphin System, Dolphin Learning Solutions, or 海豚AI学 lookalikes.
- Don’t use Atlas, Coyote, Planhaven, Tallymark, Planmoor, or other retired names as shipping names.
- Don’t lean on points, ranks, streak guilt, or parent/school surveillance UX.
- Don’t claim trademark clearance from this doc alone.
