## Locked name

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Product name | **Dolphin** |
| Category line | Learning platform / adaptive study home |
| Preferred tagline | *Learn anything. Fit the time you have. Prove you can do it.* |
| Visual direction | Clear Depth — deep water ink, seafoam action, foam surfaces, signal amber |

**Why Dolphin (product sense):** fluid movement through constrained time; depth without opacity; navigates complex waters calmly. Fits time-adaptive planning + Home as hub + honest Evidence Ledger without sounding like a generic indigo AI tutor.

## Contrast pairs (S52)

Measured with WCAG relative luminance. Body text must be ≥ 4.5:1; large text ≥ 3:1.

| Pair | Foreground | Background | Ratio | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ink on foam | `#0B1F2A` | `#F4F8F9` | 15.94:1 | Body, titles |
| Ink on elevated foam | `#0B1F2A` | `#E8F1F3` | 14.48:1 | Card body |
| Mist on foam | `#5A6B73` | `#F4F8F9` | 5.11:1 | Secondary body |
| Foam on ink | `#F4F8F9` | `#0B1F2A` | 15.94:1 | Primary button label |
| Ink on foam (kicker) | `#0B1F2A` | `#E8F1F3` | 14.48:1 | App kickers (replaces seafoam-on-foam) |
| Seafoam on foam | `#1FA7A0` | `#F4F8F9` | 2.74:1 | **Fails** body text — borders/accents only |

Kicker pills use **ink on elevated foam**, not seafoam on foam. Seafoam remains the outline accent for secondary buttons.

## Conflict screen (quick web pass)

Stay distinctive from existing “Dolphin” education products (K–12 tutoring apps, maritime LMS, B2B consulting lockups). Mitigate with **adult-first (18+)**, English-first consumer learning OS positioning, Evidence Ledger / Session Studio / real-minute plans, and counsel clearance before launch.

Avoid lockups that look like “Dolphin AI Learning”, “Dolphin System”, or “Dolphin Learning Solutions.”

**Risk level (preliminary):** Medium globally in edtech naming; mitigate with tagline + Clear Depth identity.
