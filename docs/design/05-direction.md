# 05 — Direction after the ATLAS brief

**Product:** Dolphin  
**Document role:** What to keep, what to refuse, and the phase order after the external “ATLAS” brief  
**Last updated:** September 23, 2026

An external brief described a universal learning platform under the name ATLAS, plus a time-adaptive update, plus a career-writing preface about GitHub projects. This file is the decision. It does not replace `00`–`04`.

---

## Decision

Keep building **Dolphin**. Do not rename it, do not import a second master spec, and do not restart the architecture.

The brief’s learning-science core is already the product:

- One home for any subject, any adult learner, and any real time budget.
- Personalize the route. Do not lower the evidence required to say someone can do the thing.
- Retrieval, spacing, and flexible presentation. No visual/auditory/kinesthetic diagnosis.
- A tutor may explain. It may not be the judge of mastery.
- Calendar time is not study minutes. A deadline changes priority. It does not add hours.
- Assisted work, same-session success, and a later due review are different facts.
- If the product cannot observe a skill, it says so. Listening to a lesson is not playing the guitar. A quiz is not a medical license.

The Prove Loop already demonstrates that shape for seeded Python and fractions, with no model key. Phase 1B (S41–S44) is the next honesty pass: retention only after a due independent review, that facet visible without a percent, an optional deadline that does not inflate minutes, and a snooze that does not award retention.

---

## What the brief adds, and where it lands

| Idea | Dolphin decision |
|---|---|
| Same subject, different time (15 minutes vs 2 hours vs 2 weeks vs months) | Already the planner. Keep it domain-agnostic. A new subject is a curriculum adapter, not a new app. |
| Quick Learn and a longer journey share one profile | Already true. Evidence from a short session counts in a later plan. |
| Replan when sessions are missed or a topic is already shown | Already true for budget edits. A retained or applied competency stays “already demonstrated” when the plan is rewritten. |
| Understand / apply / master as a priority | Worth a later step. It may change which competencies are in scope. It must not change what “retained” means. Do not remap the current wizard during Phase 1B. |
| “How close am I?” | Remaining competencies, deferred topics, and due reviews. Never a global percent. |
| Knowledge Vault and cited sources | Phase 2A. Owner-scoped, untrusted uploads, citations you can open. Not a reason to pause 1B. |
| Code, math, and language labs | One isolated evaluator at a time, after the ledger is honest. No in-process code execution. |
| Human study groups | After a reviewed trust and privacy design. Not V1. |
| Bayesian or FSRS schedulers | Later, and only with enough evidence to test them. Intervals stay the transparent 1 / 3 / 7 / 14 day stages until then. |
| Children and schools | A separate product review. V1 stays 18+. |

---

## What to refuse

- A second product name, tagline, or visual system. Dolphin and Clear Depth stay locked.
- Resume metrics as requirements. Invented user counts, latency drops, and coverage percents are not features. The honest proof is the Evidence Ledger and, later, work the learner produced.
- Engagement as the objective. No streaks, catch-up guilt, or leaderboards.
- A microservice fleet, Redis, Celery, or Kubernetes as a prerequisite for the next learning step. The modular monolith stays.
- A model that both teaches and writes the proficiency row.
- Marketing that every subject is fully assessed.

---

## Phase order

Finish the written steps before opening the next phase.

| Phase | Job | Starts when |
|---|---|---|
| **1B** | Delayed retention, visible facet, deadline that is not minutes, snooze that is not retention | Now. S41–S42 are on `main`. S43 then S44. |
| **1C** | Time stays honest in the UI: priority may narrow scope; goal and Home show the date beside usable minutes; missed time drops scope instead of stacking a catch-up day | After S44, as new numbered steps. Not inside 1B. |
| **1D** | Tutor gateway with seeded fallback. Hints and solutions are allowed. Evidence still comes from a different independent check. | After 1C. |
| **2A** | Knowledge Vault: private files, retrieval after an ownership filter, citations, no redistribution. | After the tutor cannot grade itself. |
| **2B** | One lab, isolated. Other domains stay labeled as unobserved until a real check exists. | After 2A’s ownership rules exist, or beside it only if the sandbox cannot touch Vault data. |

The end state is still one platform: learn anything, for anyone, in the time they actually have, with proof that is allowed to be incomplete.
